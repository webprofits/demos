Why it works

Two classes a week is why the steps stick

A class once a week is easy to forget by the next one. Two classes a week, practice in between and a video to check at home is how the steps stay in your body.

01

Two classes a week

Come to two beginner classes each week for 5 weeks. Every class builds on the last one, so you always know what you're learning next.

02

Guided practice sessions

Practise what you learned with our instructors beside you, so we fix small mistakes before they become habits.

03

Video breakdowns after class

We film each week's steps and put them in your online portal. Go over them at home as many times as you like.

04

Your first social event free

Use your new steps on a real dance floor with the people from your class. Your first event is on us.

You can come on your own. We rotate partners during the class, so you dance with lots of different people as well as our instructors and assistants. It's the fastest way to improve your lead and follow.
