Borrowing capacity

How much your SMSF can borrow

It depends on the lender. We work out your maximum borrowing amount and your maximum purchase price before you start looking.

  • What lenders countThe income your fund receives through contributions, rent and other investments.
  • Cash left in the fundSome lenders want cash or shares held after settlement, called a liquidity requirement.
  • Your maximum purchase priceYour fund's cash contribution plus the most a lender will lend to it.
Limited recourse borrowing arrangements

How an SMSF commercial loan is set up

  • Limited recourseThe lender can claim only the property securing the loan, and none of the fund's other assets.
  • The bare trustA bare trust holds the property until your fund repays the loan, then ownership passes to the fund.
  • The director's guaranteeDirectors of the trustee company sign one, so the lender can pursue their personal assets for a shortfall.
  • The depositYour fund pays it from its own bank account, and the lender asks for proof of the cash.
  • The repaymentsRent, contributions and your fund's other investment income pay the loan.
  • The documentsTrust deeds, SMSF financials, tax returns, audits, the contract of sale and SMSF bank statements.

Questions before you call

Can my business lease the property from my fund?

Yes. If you run your own business, your business is able to lease a commercial property from your SMSF. Your accountant or financial adviser can tell you what that means for your tax and for your fund.

My SMSF is new and the rollover isn't done. Can I still apply?

Yes. If your funds haven't rolled over yet, you can show that your current super fund holds the required amount. Lenders accept that as proof of the cash contribution.

Who advises on whether my fund should buy?

Your accountant or financial adviser, and it's the first step. We arrange the lending and work with them on borrowing limits, repayments and the cash your fund keeps.

What happens between application and settlement?

Once we submit the loan, the process is the same as for any other loan. When the lender approves it, we work with your solicitor or conveyancer, the lender and the seller's solicitor so settlement happens on the date required.
