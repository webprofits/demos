Common questions

What business owners ask before buying premises

How much deposit do I need for a commercial property loan?

Commercial property loans usually start with a minimum deposit of 30%. It can come from cash, from equity in property you already own, or from funds in your SMSF. The feasibility check works out which sources suit your purchase.

My bank said no. Is it still worth asking?

Yes. Different lenders have different requirements, so a purchase that doesn't fit one lender may fit another. We compare 40+ lenders, including non-bank lenders and private options, and we're realistic about what may or may not work.

Can my SMSF buy the premises my business runs from?

It can. Your SMSF buys the property and is able to lease it to your business. The lending rules are complex, so we help you understand lender requirements and borrowing limits before you commit. Speak to your accountant or financial adviser first.

Should I sort out finance before I make an offer?

Yes. Commercial vendors often won't accept an offer that's subject to finance. Once you sign the contract, you risk losing your deposit if you can't complete the sale. We aim to have an agreement in principle from a lender before you offer.
