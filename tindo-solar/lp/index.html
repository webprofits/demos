Questions

What Adelaide homeowners ask before they get a quote

How much does a solar system cost?

Every home is different, so we quote each system instead of selling set packages. Your free quote sets out the system, the price and any rebate you qualify for.

What size system do I need?

A 6.6kW system is enough for most South Australian households. We’ll look at how much power you use and when, then size the system to cover you all year.

How long does installation take?

Most installs take one day. System size, access and weather can change that, and your energy consultant confirms the timing when they inspect your home.

Which way should my panels face?

North is best, because north facing panels usually get the most sun. North east and north west also work. In Adelaide the recommended tilt is around 30 degrees.

How much power does a 10kW system make?

On a clear sunny day you can expect a 10kW system to produce around 40kWh. Weather changes that, and every home is different.

Am I eligible for the federal battery rebate?

You may qualify if you own or rent a home and have solar, or add it with the battery. The battery has to be an approved one that’s Virtual Power Plant ready. We’ll confirm it in your quote.

What does the 25 year warranty cover?

Tindo panels carry a 25 year product warranty from the factory in Mawson Lakes. The $1,000 Premium Warranty Offer adds cover for parts, transport, removal and reinstallation if a panel malfunctions.

What is Pay As You Save?

It lets you pay for your system over time instead of upfront, through our finance partner Plenti. It’s subject to approval by the lender.
