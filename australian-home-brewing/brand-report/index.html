Banned claim categories

  • Claims that distilling spirits at home for personal consumption is lawful without a permit, or that the permit requirement can be ignored (misleading conduct under the ACL; Excise Act exposure).
  • Instructions or step-by-step prompts to distil drinking alcohol in ad, email or social copy.
  • Any claim of selling, gifting for sale, or commercial resale of home-produced alcohol being allowed.
  • Health, therapeutic or wellness claims for any product (outside TGA scope, and prohibited by Meta and Google alcohol policies for this kind of product).
  • Safety guarantees that overstate protection (for example implying a still or keg cannot fail).
