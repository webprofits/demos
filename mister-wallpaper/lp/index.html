The difference

What's the difference between wallpaper and a wallpaper mural?

Wallpaper is a pattern that repeats. A wallpaper mural is one picture, made for one wall. That changes everything about how it's designed, printed and hung.

Wallpaper

Chosen from a catalogue, bought by the roll.

Design
A repeating pattern someone else drew. The same roll is on walls all over the country.
Joins
Narrow rolls, so a wide wall means many joins and matching the repeat at every one.
Fit
Cut to the wall after the fact. Whatever lands at the corners, lands there.
Your brand
Your logo, your colours and your city are not an option.
vs

Wallpaper mural

One image, printed to your wall's exact size.

Design
One artwork for one wall: your logo, your colours, a photo, an illustration or a map of your city.
Joins
Panels up to 125 cm wide, printed to your measurements. An 8 metre wall has a handful of joins and the picture runs straight through them.
Fit
Designed to the wall before it prints, with 10 cm of bleed, so the focal point sits where you want it.
Your brand
Built around your logo and brand colours, checked by a person at full size, then installed by the team that printed it.

How Mister Wallpaper does it

One team from the artwork check to the last trim, which is why the finish holds up

Every commercial job is treated as its own brief. Here's what that means on your site.

Your artwork, checked by a person before it prints

Logo, brand colours, campaign imagery or a photo you supply. Every file is checked for resolution at full wall size before it goes to print. If there's any doubt, you hear about it first. Promised.

Printed to the wall, not to a roll

Each mural is printed to your wall's measurements on PRO wallpaper panels up to 125 cm wide, so an 8 metre reception wall has a handful of joins, not a dozen.

Commercial-grade, PVC-free material

PRO wallpaper is wet-strength, fade-resistant and recyclable, with a satin finish that's easy to maintain in a busy office, clinic or hotel corridor. It removes with water when the fit-out changes, with no damage to the wall.

Installed by our team or our installer network

Sydney, Wollongong, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth, with installation support in many other locations. Print turnaround is 5 to 7 business days, with a 3 day express option when the handover date is fixed.
